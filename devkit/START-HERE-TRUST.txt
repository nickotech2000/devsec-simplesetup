 WHAT THIS KIT DOES
   It makes your computer trust the private PKI of the company: the root and intermediate CAs.
   It holds NO private key. It cannot make certificates, sign requests (CSR) or sign code: the
   generators, SignCsr and Sign-Code are not in this kit. Ask the owner of the PKI for those.
 The full manual is docs\DevKit-MANUAL.txt. Words are explained in docs\GLOSSARY.txt.

 THE PASSWORD of the package comes from the person who sent you this kit, by another way.
 It is not in the kit.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WINDOWS   (Windows 11 24H2 or newer; Windows Sandbox too)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Step 1   Unzip the kit (right-click, Extract All) to a short folder, for example C:\pki-trust.
 Step 2   Open Windows PowerShell AS ADMINISTRATOR in that folder. Free THIS window only:
              Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Force
              Get-ChildItem -Recurse -File | Unblock-File
 Step 3   No PowerShell 7 or OpenSSL 4 yet (always so in Windows Sandbox):
              .\Install-DevEnvironment.ps1 -Only PowerShell,OpenSSL,7Zip
 Step 4   Install the trust:
              pwsh -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Install-PkiTrust.ps1
          It asks for the password, unpacks the kit and installs the CAs.
 More, also for a Windows Sandbox: RUN-SCRIPTS-WINDOWS.txt

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 LINUX / UBUNTU
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Step 1   unzip agantra-pki-devkit-*.zip && cd agantra-pki-devkit-*
 Step 2   bash make-executable.sh
 Step 3   ./Install-PkiTrust.sh            (sudo is asked only for the system store)

 GOOD TO KNOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * Installing again removes the older CAs of this same PKI first (only of this PKI, matched by
   thumbprint) and puts the new ones in. Public CAs and other PKIs are never touched.
 * Trouble? docs\DevKit-MANUAL.txt, section 15, lists the messages and the fixes.
